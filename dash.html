Second Trimester (Weeks 13-27)

The second trimester is often called the "golden period" of pregnancy. Many women experience a decrease in morning sickness and fatigue, and an increase in energy levels.

Your baby is growing rapidly during this time. By week 24, your baby is about 30 centimeters long and weighs around 600 grams. Their facial features are becoming more defined, and they can hear sounds from outside the womb.

This is also when you'll likely start feeling your baby's movements, typically between weeks 18-22. These movements may feel like flutters at first, gradually becoming more pronounced as your baby grows.
