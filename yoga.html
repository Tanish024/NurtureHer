Cat-Cow Flow

Gentle Spinal Warm-up

  • Improves spinal flexibility
  • Relieves lower back tension
  • Enhances breathing awareness

Instructions:

Start on hands and knees. Inhale arch back (cow), exhale round spine (cat). Repeat 8-10 times.

Pelvic Tilts

Core Strengthening

  • Strengthens abdominal muscles
  • Improves posture alignment
  • Prepares for labor positions

Instructions:

Lie on your back with knees bent. Gently tilt your pelvis upward, hold for 3 breaths. Repeat 10 times.

Supported Squat

Hip Opening Pose

  • Strengthens leg muscles
  • Increases hip flexibility
  • Improves birth positioning

Instructions:

Use yoga blocks for support. Hold the squat position for 30 seconds, repeat 3 times.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is prenatal yoga safe during all trimesters?

Yes, our classes are designed to be safe for all trimesters. However, we recommend consulting with your healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program during pregnancy.

Do I need prior yoga experience?

No prior experience is necessary. Our classes are suitable for beginners and experienced practitioners alike.

What should I bring to class?

Please bring a yoga mat, comfortable clothing, water, and any props you might need for comfort (though we provide basic props).

Do you offer virtual classes?

Yes, we offer both in-person and virtual classes to accommodate all mothers-to-be. Contact us for more information about our virtual options.
