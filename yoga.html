Safe Prenatal Yoga for Mother & Baby

Cat-Cow Flow

Gentle Spinal Warm-up

  • Improves spinal flexibility
  • Relieves lower back tension
  • Enhances breathing awareness

Instructions:

Start on hands and knees. Inhale arch back (cow), exhale round spine (cat). Repeat 8-10 times.

Pelvic Tilts

Core Strengthening

  • Strengthens abdominal muscles
  • Improves posture alignment
  • Prepares for labor positions

Instructions:

Lie on back with knees bent. Gently tilt pelvis upward, hold for 3 breaths. Repeat 10 times.

Supported Squat

Hip Opening Pose

  • Strengthens leg muscles
  • Increases hip flexibility
  • Improves birth positioning

Instructions:

Use yoga blocks for support. Hold squat position for 30 seconds, repeat 3 times.
