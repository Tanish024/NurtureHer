Benefits of Donating Blood

Beyond saving lives, blood donation offers several health benefits for donors.

Reduces Risk of Heart Disease

Regular blood donation can reduce the risk of heart attacks and strokes by reducing iron levels in the body.

Free Health Check-up

Each donation includes a mini health screening that checks blood pressure, pulse, and hemoglobin levels.

Burns Calories

Donating one pint of blood burns approximately 650 calories as your body works to replace the blood volume.

Stimulates Blood Cell Production

After donating, your body replaces the blood volume within 48 hours, stimulating the production of new blood cells.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about blood donation.

Who can donate blood?

Generally, donors must be at least 18 years old (16-17 with parental consent in some areas), weigh at least 110 pounds, and be in good health. Specific eligibility criteria may vary based on local regulations.

How often can I donate blood?

Whole blood donors can donate every 56 days (about 8 weeks). Platelet donors can donate more frequently, as often as every 7 days, up to 24 times per year.

Is blood donation painful?

Most donors report feeling only a brief pinch when the needle is inserted. The actual donation process is typically painless. Our trained staff will ensure your comfort throughout the process.

How long does the donation process take?

The entire process takes about an hour, including registration, health screening, donation, and refreshments. The actual blood draw typically takes only 8-10 minutes.

What should I do before donating blood?

Get a good night's sleep, eat a healthy meal, drink plenty of fluids, and avoid fatty foods before donating. Bring a photo ID and a list of medications you're taking.
